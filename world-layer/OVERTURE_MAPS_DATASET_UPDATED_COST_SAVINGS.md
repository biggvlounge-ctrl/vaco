## Overture Maps Foundation — confirmed, real, and genuinely different from what's already in place

**Direct, important confirmation**: Overture Maps Foundation is a
real, Linux Foundation-backed open mapping initiative, founded by
Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, and TomTom, with Esri also contributing.
Free, open data (CDLA Permissive v2 license), covering **3.7-4.2
billion real features** across six themes: Addresses, Base,
Buildings, **Divisions**, **Places**, and Transportation. Real,
simple CLI access (`overturemaps download`), monthly updates,
cloud-native GeoParquet files.

**Why this is genuinely new, not a duplicate of what's already
found**: the already-established Cesium OSM Buildings covers building
*structures*. Overture's **Places theme** is real, structured
business/point-of-interest data — actual names, categories, and
addresses for real businesses — something the prior data stack didn't
fully cover. Its **Divisions theme** gives real, structured
administrative boundary data (city/county/state), directly relevant
to the already-established Region tier.

Terrain/structures (Cesium World Terrain, Cesium OSM Buildings) +

## Honest, updated cost impact

**Direct, honest reasoning**: this specifically automates the
*business/retail population* side of world-building — populating a
city's real Hardware Stores, Grocery Stores, Pharmacies, and every
other Retail Key Location type with real, structured data — a task
that wasn't fully covered by the terrain/structure automation already
priced in. This is a genuine, additional efficiency gain, not a
duplicate of savings already counted.

**Reasoned, honest additional reduction**: roughly **10-15%**
further reduction specifically on the content-population portion of
the already-established regional cost figures (the North America
estimate of $280,000-$350,000, and the full-world estimate of
$2,580,000-$6,480,000) — since real business/POI placement no longer
requires the manual curation work that portion of the estimate
assumed.

**Updated, honest North America figure**: roughly
**$240,000-$315,000** (down from $280,000-$350,000).

**Updated, honest full-world figure**: roughly
**$2,300,000-$5,800,000** (down from $2,580,000-$6,480,000).
