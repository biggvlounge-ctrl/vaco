# VXLLAGE — Social Platform Comparables

Written to close a comparables-coverage gap.

**What it is:** feed, threads, villages (topic communities), profiles,
follows, search, and a cross-village avatar cosmetics shop.

## Comparables

| Platform | Structure | Monetization |
|---|---|---|
| **Reddit** | Subreddits — the closest structural analogue to villages: user-created topic communities with their own moderation. | Advertising, Premium, awards |
| **Discord** | Servers with channels; stronger identity persistence across communities. | Nitro subscription, **cosmetics and profile customization** |
| **Nextdoor** | Geography-bound communities rather than topic-bound. | Local advertising |
| **X / Mastodon** | Follow-graph feed rather than community-first. | Subscription / donation |

## Where VXLLAGE actually differs

**Discord is the most relevant comparable, and specifically its
monetization.** Discord proved that cosmetics and identity
customization can carry a social platform without advertising —
avoiding the incentive structure that made Reddit's and X's
relationships with their users adversarial.

VXLLAGE's cross-village avatar shop is the same bet, with one real
advantage: the avatar is not confined to VXLLAGE. It is the same
identity that walks through VDP's districts. A cosmetic bought here is
visible across the ecosystem, which is a materially stronger purchase
than a Discord profile banner.

**Not selling advertising is the strategically important part**, and
worth stating explicitly because DREAMS exists and the temptation is
obvious. Every social comparable that monetized through ads ended up
optimizing the feed for engagement rather than for the people in it.
The ecosystem already has an ad business; VXLLAGE does not need to
become a second one.

## The unavoidable cost

Community platforms need moderation, and it is not optional at any
scale above trivial. Reddit relies on volunteer moderators, Discord on
server owners, Nextdoor on a mix. **VXLLAGE has villages but no
moderation model in code** — no roles, no removal, no reporting.

That is the single largest gap between this app and every comparable
listed, and it is the one that becomes urgent the moment there are real
users rather than at some later scale.

## What is genuinely built

Feed, threads, villages, profiles, follows, search, avatar cosmetics
with real purchases settling through V3, and a VDP village district.
19 modules, 64 routes, persisted.
