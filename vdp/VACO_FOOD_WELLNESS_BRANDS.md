## The nine brands

### VIVE — Coffee & Fresh-Pressed Beverages
Premium coffee, fresh-pressed juices, wellness drinks, and bottled
water. A flagship VDP destination, also extending to real cafés,
lounges, VOID Hubs, and retail distribution.
**Products**: fresh-pressed juices, premium coffee, wellness drinks,
bottled water.

### VIXENS — Vegan Restaurant
A modern vegan comfort food restaurant offering healthy, flavorful
plant-based meals — a real, signature dining destination within VDP's
Food District, extending to real-world dining, delivery, and catering.
**Products**: vegan comfort food, vegan entrées, plant-based meals,
healthy meal options.

### VORDABELLO'S — Upscale Chef-Made Italian, Fast
**Corrected positioning**: not a standard Italian restaurant —
upscale, chef-made Italian cuisine, comparable to Olive Garden's
quality tier, but delivered at fast/quick-service speed. Real, smart
economics: genuinely low actual food cost (pizza, pasta, and Italian
staples are inexpensive to produce) paired with a premium, chef-made
perception — a high-margin positioning, not a contradiction.
**Products**: pizza, pasta, Italian sandwiches, chef-made Italian
entrées.

### VAZAN — Supplements & Skincare
A health and wellness brand focused on premium nutritional
supplements, skincare products, and personal wellness solutions — a
flagship wellness destination within VDP.
**Products**: vitamins, nutritional supplements, skincare products,
wellness essentials.

### VODEGA — Full-Variety Sandwich Shop, Hot & Cold
**Corrected positioning**: a broad, basic-operation sandwich shop
covering both hot and cold sandwich options across many different
varieties, plus fries and chips — variety and operational simplicity
are the actual draw, not an upscale angle. Real, efficient, high-
throughput model.
**Products**: hot sandwiches, cold sandwiches, fries, chips, varied
sandwich options.

### VFRESH — Fresh Produce & Grocery Goods
Fresh, quality produce and grocery goods — a flagship fresh-goods
destination in VDP, distinct from the other five as raw, unprepared
goods rather than prepared meals or wellness products.
**Products**: fresh vegetables, fresh fruit, fresh grocery goods.

### TACO TOWN — Taco Spot
A dedicated taco concept — real, quick-service Mexican-inspired food,
a flagship taco destination in VDP's Food District.
**Products**: tacos, real Mexican-inspired sides and fillings (to be
detailed further).

### BIG JACK'S — Burger Spot
**Naming note**: originally proposed as "Jack's," self-corrected to
"Big Jack's." Good instinct — worth a quick, honest trademark

national brand. "Big Jack's" is meaningfully more distinct, but a
quick trademark search before final commitment is worth doing given
how common "Jack's" is in the food industry.
A real, flagship burger destination in VDP's Food District.
**Products**: burgers, real burger-spot sides (to be detailed
further).

### NETTY'S — Soul Food
A genuine soul food restaurant concept — the ninth flagship brand,
resolving the previously open item. A real, distinct dining
destination in VDP's Food District, rounding out the portfolio's
range across a real, beloved American food tradition not yet
represented among the other eight brands.
**Products**: soul food entrées and sides (to be detailed further —
real specific menu items to be confirmed).

## Confirmed: two-location kitchen grouping, not one shared space for all six

Multi-brand ghost kitchens are real, standard industry practice
(CloudKitchens, Kitchen United both built around this exact model) —
running multiple concepts from shared kitchen space to spread fixed
costs. Confirmed real-world split, chosen deliberately rather than
grouping all six together:

**Location 1 — VIXENS, VFRESH, VIVE**: a genuinely complementary
grouping — VFRESH's raw produce directly supplies both VIXENS' vegan
dishes and VIVE's fresh-pressed juices (real ingredient-sharing
efficiency, not just shared space). All three are fresh, plant-forward
concepts, which also cleanly avoids any cross-contamination concern
with meat-based prep.

**Location 2 — VORDABELLO'S, VODEGA**: also genuinely complementary —
Italian sandwiches and traditional deli sandwiches share real prep
equipment (bread, deli stations, ovens); both are traditional
prepared-food concepts.

**VAZAN — separate consideration**: not placed in any kitchen group,
since it's a non-kitchen product (supplements/skincare) requiring its
own storage/handling space regardless of which physical location it's
paired with.

**Location 3 — TACO TOWN, NETTY'S, BIG JACK'S**: confirmed. A third,
real kitchen grouping for the three newer brands — tacos, soul food,
and burgers together, distinct from Location 1's fresh/plant-forward
group and Location 2's Italian/sandwich group. All three are hearty,
hot-prepared, protein-forward concepts, a genuinely coherent grouping
in the same spirit as the first two locations.
