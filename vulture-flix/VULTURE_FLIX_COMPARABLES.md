# Vvltvre Flix — Streaming Video Comparables

Written to close a comparables-coverage gap.

**What it is:** titles, three subscription tiers, and licensed
non-exclusive content alongside originals.

## Comparables

| Platform | Model |
|---|---|
| **Netflix** | Subscription tiers including an ad-supported one; originals plus licensed catalogue. The model Vvltvre Flix's tier structure most resembles. |
| **Max** | Same shape, with a deeper owned back catalogue. |
| **Hulu** | Ad-supported and ad-free tiers; strong on next-day broadcast licensing. |
| **Tubi** | **Free, entirely ad-supported (AVOD)**, built on licensed catalogue rather than originals. |

## The honest read

Netflix is the obvious comparison and the wrong target. It is the
single most expensive content business in the world, and licensed
non-exclusive catalogue — which is what Vvltvre Flix actually has —
does not differentiate against it, because anyone can license the same
titles.

**Tubi is the more instructive comparable.** It built a real business
on exactly the position Vvltvre Flix is in: licensed non-exclusive
content, no original-production budget, monetized through advertising
rather than asking consumers to add another subscription to a stack
they are already trimming.

That points somewhere concrete: DREAMS already exists as ad
infrastructure inside the ecosystem. An ad-supported tier served by the
ecosystem's own ad network is a structurally cheaper position than
competing on subscription against Netflix — and it is the kind of
cross-app leverage that is genuinely hard for a standalone competitor
to copy.

**Stated as the open question it is**, not a recommendation: the three
built tiers assume a subscription business. Whether that or an AVOD
position is right is a strategy decision, and it should be made
deliberately rather than inherited from the tier code already written.

## What is genuinely built

Titles, three subscription tiers, licensed non-exclusive title support,
VDP embed, feeding VACO Analytics. Persisted.

## Not covered here

Video delivery. Same real-time media gap as Vavlt Stvdios — the
economics are modeled, the transport is not built.
