# VENVS / VDP — Digital Planet Economy Comparables (v1)

## Virtual land ownership — Decentraland/The Sandbox model
Both run on real blockchain-based land NFTs (Decentraland's LAND on
Ethereum, The Sandbox's SAND-denominated parcels on a layer-2 for lower
fees), DAO governance where landholders vote on platform decisions, and
a real secondary economy: land rental/leasing to brands and event
organizers is now more common than land flipping (a real market
maturity signal — the 2026 sources note this shift explicitly). This
directly validates VENVS's own Section 48 ownership ladder and Digital
Twin concept — land-as-real-asset with rental yield is a proven,
current model, not a speculative one.

## Avatar economy — Roblox is the real, current playbook
Roblox ran a major UGC avatar overhaul across Jan–June 2026: retiring
static 2D avatar faces for fully animated "Dynamic Heads," raising the
cost floor to publish avatar items, and launching new paid categories
like **Avatar Makeup — real-brand-sponsored (e.l.f. Cosmetics as
launch sponsor, NYX following)**. The stated business driver: Roblox's
18-34 adult cohort is growing 50%+ year-over-year and monetizes 50%+
higher than under-18 players, and Roblox's Developer Exchange payout
bonus (42%) now specifically favors the modern avatar system over the
legacy one.

## Direct translation to VENVS and DEGVCHI specifically
