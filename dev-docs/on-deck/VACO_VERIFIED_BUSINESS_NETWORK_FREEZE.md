VACO shall evolve from an internal corporate ecosystem into a scalable verified business economic network capable of supporting VACO-owned companies and independently owned external businesses.

• Verified businesses
• Business Passports

## 1. VACO VERIFIED BUSINESS NETWORK

VACO shall support one shared network capable of serving:

• VACO-owned companies
• VACO-affiliated companies
• Independently owned external businesses
• Vendors
• Merchants
• Service providers
• Partners
• Developers
• Other qualifying commercial participants

External businesses do not become VACO-owned merely because they join the network.

The network establishes technological connectivity, verification, transaction relationships and auditable records.

Corporate ownership remains a separate legal matter.

The architecture must support progression from:

100+ businesses
→ 1,000+ businesses
→ 10,000+ businesses
→ substantially larger future network populations.

Do not create a separate blockchain, application stack or isolated network for every company.

Use one scalable network with strong tenant isolation and controlled interoperability.

## 2. FIVE-TIER BUSINESS NETWORK

LEVEL 1 — NETWORK MEMBER

A business may participate without tokenization.

Capabilities may include:

• Business account
• Business profile
• VACO services
• Marketplace participation
• VDP participation
• HUNTZ participation where applicable
• VENVS participation where applicable
• Network transactions
• Basic network relationships

LEVEL 2 — VERIFIED BUSINESS

VACA verifies the business.

The business receives:

• Verified business identity
• Business Passport
• Authorized representatives
• Verification status
• Business wallet/account
• Network permissions
• Connected VACO services
• Auditable network identity

LEVEL 3 — NETWORK BUSINESS

The verified business actively conducts commerce through VACO.

Capabilities may include:

• Network transactions
• VCoin participation
• VASH integration where available/applicable
• Escrow
• Business-to-business settlement
• Contracts
• Vendor/customer relationships
• Network APIs
• Transaction history
• Network services
• Developer integrations

LEVEL 4 — TOKENIZED BUSINESS

A qualifying verified business may use VOKEN to establish a company-specific digital token structure.

The token must have clearly defined legal and economic rights.

A token does NOT automatically represent corporate ownership.

Tokenization must follow applicable legal, regulatory and compliance requirements.

VOKEN should support:

Define
→ Classify
→ Legal/Compliance Review
→ Configure
→ Approve
→ Mint/Issue
→ Distribute
→ Transfer
→ Track
→ Retire

LEVEL 5 — TOKENIZED ASSETS

A qualifying business may tokenize legally defined interests in eligible assets, projects or economic arrangements.

Examples may include:

• Real estate
• Development projects
• Business assets
• Membership structures
• Revenue/economic interests
• Project participation
• Other legally structured assets

The underlying legal entity, contracts and applicable regulations determine the actual rights.

Blockchain records the digital representation and transaction history.

• Authorized representatives

• Network membership level

## 5. INVESTOR-READY BUSINESS OBJECTIVE

A long-term strategic objective of the VACO Verified Business Network is to help participating businesses become increasingly INVESTOR-READY.

VACO should create a verified and auditable record of legitimate business activity.

The network should allow an authorized business, investor, lender, partner or other qualified party to evaluate appropriate verified records subject to permissions, privacy, confidentiality, applicable law and contractual requirements.

The verified business record may include:

• Business identity
• Network tenure
• Revenue/settlement history where available
• Verified transactions
• Completed contracts
• Customer/vendor activity
• Fulfillment history
• Assets
• Eligible economic interests
• Escrow history
• Network relationships
• Business growth/activity indicators
• Tokenization records
• Asset records

The VACO ledger is NOT a guarantee of financing, investment or business success.

The ledger is intended to provide supporting evidence and BUSINESS-DATA LEVERAGE that a business may use when pursuing legitimate:

• Investment
• Financing
• Lending
• Partnerships
• Contracts
• Expansion capital
• Other commercial opportunities

The long-term vision is for businesses to build a verifiable economic history that remains associated with their Business Passport.

## 10. VCOIN VALIDATION / NETWORK INFRASTRUCTURE

If VCoin becomes blockchain-based, VACO may operate or coordinate network validation infrastructure.

Do not assume traditional proof-of-work mining is required.

Evaluate appropriate architectures including:

• Permissioned validation
• Proof-of-stake or similar models
• Distributed validators
• VACO-operated nodes
• Qualified external validators
• Hybrid network models

The term "mining" should be treated carefully.

The intended VACO role is primarily:

NETWORK OPERATOR
+
NETWORK INFRASTRUCTURE PROVIDER
+
VALIDATION PARTICIPANT WHERE APPROPRIATE
+
TOKENIZATION INFRASTRUCTURE PROVIDER
+
BUSINESS NETWORK OPERATOR

VACO should not automatically create thousands of independent business cryptocurrencies.

VCoin is the network-level asset.

Individual business tokens, when appropriate, are handled through VOKEN.

## 19. VACO COMMUNITY COMMERCE PROGRAM

VACO shall support a community-oriented commerce model.

Qualifying VACO Community Stores shall allocate a defined percentage of eligible proceeds to community benefit within a defined geographic service area.

INITIAL TARGET:

25% COMMUNITY ALLOCATION

The precise financial definition of the 25% must be established operationally and legally.

Do not automatically assume the percentage is 25% of gross revenue.

The Master Freeze must allow the exact eligible economic base to be configured and documented.

## 21. VOID HUB COMMUNITY DIVIDEND

EVERY QUALIFYING VOID HUB shall establish its own local community allocation.

INITIAL TARGET:

25% COMMUNITY ALLOCATION
