## PURPOSE

VACO shall evolve from an internal corporate ecosystem into a scalable
verified business economic network capable of supporting VACO-owned
companies and independently owned external businesses.

The long-term architecture shall support: verified businesses, Business
Passports, multi-tenant business infrastructure, business wallets,
VCoin, VASH, V3, VOKEN, smart-contract infrastructure, business-to-
business commerce, escrow, network settlement, external APIs, the VACO
Business Network SDK, the VACO Interoperability Gateway, blockchain-
based verification, potential future VCoin cryptocurrency
functionality, tokenized businesses, tokenized assets, community
economic participation, community transparency ledgers, and
investor-readiness through verified business history.

The fundamental principle is: **BUSINESSES DO NOT JUMP DIRECTLY TO
TOKENIZATION.** They graduate through the verified network.

NETWORK MEMBER → VERIFIED BUSINESS → NETWORK BUSINESS → TOKENIZED
BUSINESS → TOKENIZED ASSETS

All new functionality must integrate with existing VACO infrastructure.
**DO NOT DUPLICATE EXISTING VACO SYSTEMS.** Before implementing
anything, inspect the existing codebase and identify what already
exists. Reuse and extend existing implementations for: VACA, VASH,
VCoin, V3, VOKEN, VACO Shell, VOID, VDP, HUNTZ, VENVS, V4, VACON, QVAN,
authentication, identity, wallets, notifications, search, AI, analytics,
APIs, testing, design system, security, audit systems, and other
completed VACO infrastructure. Build only missing functionality.

## 1. VACO VERIFIED BUSINESS NETWORK

One shared network serving VACO-owned companies, VACO-affiliated
companies, independently owned external businesses, vendors, merchants,
service providers, partners, developers and other qualifying commercial
participants.

External businesses do not become VACO-owned merely by joining. The
network establishes technological connectivity, verification,
transaction relationships and auditable records. Corporate ownership
remains a separate legal matter.

The architecture must support 100+ → 1,000+ → 10,000+ → substantially
larger populations. Do not create a separate blockchain, application
stack or isolated network per company. Use one scalable network with
strong tenant isolation and controlled interoperability.

## 2. FIVE-TIER BUSINESS NETWORK

**LEVEL 1 — NETWORK MEMBER.** Participate without tokenization:
business account, business profile, VACO services, marketplace, VDP,
HUNTZ where applicable, VENVS where applicable, network transactions,
basic network relationships.

**LEVEL 2 — VERIFIED BUSINESS.** VACA verifies the business. Receives
verified business identity, Business Passport, authorized
representatives, verification status, business wallet/account, network
permissions, connected VACO services, auditable network identity.

**LEVEL 3 — NETWORK BUSINESS.** Actively conducts commerce: network
transactions, VCoin participation, VASH integration where applicable,
escrow, business-to-business settlement, contracts, vendor/customer
relationships, network APIs, transaction history, network services,
developer integrations.

**LEVEL 4 — TOKENIZED BUSINESS.** A qualifying verified business may
use VOKEN to establish a company-specific digital token structure with
clearly defined legal and economic rights. A token does NOT
automatically represent corporate ownership. Tokenization must follow
applicable legal, regulatory and compliance requirements. VOKEN should
support: Define → Classify → Legal/Compliance Review → Configure →
Approve → Mint/Issue → Distribute → Transfer → Track → Retire.

**LEVEL 5 — TOKENIZED ASSETS.** A qualifying business may tokenize
legally defined interests in eligible assets, projects or economic
arrangements — real estate, development projects, business assets,
membership structures, revenue/economic interests, project
participation, other legally structured assets. The underlying legal
entity, contracts and applicable regulations determine the actual
rights. Blockchain records the digital representation and transaction
history.

## 3. VACA BUSINESS PASSPORT

The business equivalent of the VACA identity layer. May contain: legal
business identity, registration information, ownership/authority
information where appropriate, authorized representatives, verification
status, applicable licenses, compliance/documentation status, business
wallet references, network membership level, network permissions,
connected VACO services, transaction history references, contract
references, tokenization status, asset records, network relationships,
audit references.

The Business Passport must persist as the business progresses through
levels. Businesses must NOT have to recreate identity, wallet, history
or account when advancing.

## 4. BUSINESS TRUST / REPUTATION HISTORY

An objective, auditable business-history layer. **Do NOT create an
arbitrary subjective "credit score."** Track objective network activity:
verified transactions, fulfilled contracts, completed orders, vendor
relationships, customer relationships, dispute records, resolution
records, network tenure, settlement history, escrow history, compliance
events, other objectively verifiable network activity.

## 5. INVESTOR-READY BUSINESS OBJECTIVE

A verified and auditable record of legitimate business activity,
evaluable by an authorized business, investor, lender, partner or other
qualified party subject to permissions, privacy, confidentiality,
applicable law and contractual requirements.

May include: business identity, network tenure, revenue/settlement
history where available, verified transactions, completed contracts,
customer/vendor activity, fulfillment history, assets, eligible
economic interests, escrow history, network relationships, business
growth/activity indicators, tokenization records, asset records.

**The VACO ledger is NOT a guarantee of financing, investment or
business success.** It provides supporting evidence and BUSINESS-DATA
LEVERAGE for pursuing legitimate investment, financing, lending,
partnerships, contracts, expansion capital and other commercial
opportunities.

## 6. BUSINESS WALLET ARCHITECTURE

Separate business financial/account structures where appropriate:
operating funds, VCoin, escrow, tokenized assets, project-specific
wallets, treasury, other controlled business accounts.

Role-based authorization. **Employees must not automatically control
company treasury.** Support treasury permissions, employee permissions,
role permissions, transaction approval, wallet authorization,
multi-party authorization where appropriate, audit trails. Reuse
existing VACO wallet and authorization infrastructure wherever possible.

## 7. V3 ECONOMIC LEDGER

V3 remains the VACO economic/financial ledger, recording transactions,
revenue, expenses, settlement, fees, escrow, business relationships,
network activity, community allocations and other economic events, and
remains integrated with VCoin, VASH, VOKEN and the wider network.

## 8. VCOIN NETWORK ECONOMIC LAYER

VCoin remains the common VACO economic/settlement layer, usable across
applicable VACO businesses and network transactions.

Long-term architecture must allow VCoin to evolve from an
internal/network economic unit into a blockchain-based
cryptocurrency/native digital asset if legally, financially,
technically and strategically approved. **DO NOT build a temporary
VCoin architecture that makes future blockchain conversion
unnecessarily difficult.** Architect a clean path toward blockchain
transactions, cryptographic wallets, on-chain records, network
validation, issuance controls, supply management, treasury
architecture, transfer infrastructure, settlement, auditing and
external interoperability.

## 9. VCOIN CRYPTOCURRENCY FUTURE PATH

VACO businesses → real economic activity → V3 economic records → VCoin
transactions → blockchain verification → external network
interoperability.

The blockchain should complement existing VACO ledgers rather than
replace them:

VACO BUSINESS LEDGER (what business activity occurred?) ↓ V3 (what was
the economic/financial representation?) ↓ VCOIN BLOCKCHAIN (what
digital-asset transaction occurred?)

The blockchain should generally NOT contain confidential source
documents or unnecessary personally identifiable information. Sensitive
information stays within controlled VACO infrastructure. On-chain
records may use transaction IDs, hashes, public identifiers, proofs,
timestamps, wallet addresses and authorized metadata.

## 10. VCOIN VALIDATION / NETWORK INFRASTRUCTURE

If VCoin becomes blockchain-based, VACO may operate or coordinate
network validation infrastructure. **Do not assume traditional
proof-of-work mining is required.** Evaluate permissioned validation,
proof-of-stake or similar, distributed validators, VACO-operated nodes,
qualified external validators, hybrid models. The term "mining" should
be treated carefully.

VACO's intended role: NETWORK OPERATOR + NETWORK INFRASTRUCTURE
PROVIDER + VALIDATION PARTICIPANT WHERE APPROPRIATE + TOKENIZATION
INFRASTRUCTURE PROVIDER + BUSINESS NETWORK OPERATOR.

VACO should not automatically create thousands of independent business
cryptocurrencies. VCoin is the network-level asset; individual business
tokens go through VOKEN.

## 11. VOKEN TOKEN FACTORY

Controlled token issuance infrastructure. A verified business should not
need to become a blockchain developer. Workflow: DEFINE →
LEGAL/COMPLIANCE CLASSIFICATION → CONFIGURE → REVIEW → APPROVE →
ISSUE/MINT → DISTRIBUTE → MANAGE → TRANSFER → TRACK → RETIRE.
Different classifications must have different controls. Token rights
must be explicit.

## 12. SMART-CONTRACT TEMPLATES

Controlled templates for business membership, vendor agreements,
escrow, marketplace settlement, revenue distribution, asset
participation, fractional interests, event/ticket structures,
membership structures and other legally approved commercial
arrangements. **Do not allow unrestricted smart-contract creation to
bypass VACO compliance and security controls.**

## 13. NETWORK SETTLEMENT ENGINE

A settlement orchestration layer connecting VACO businesses and
systems. Customer → VDP → Business A → VACO → Business B → VOID.
Applicable systems maintain identity, transaction, economic,
settlement, contract, escrow, audit and network relationship records.

Customer buys through VDP. VDP records commerce. VACA establishes
participant identity. V3 records the economic event. VCoin handles
applicable network settlement. VASH handles applicable
financial/banking activity. VOID handles applicable logistics.
Blockchain records applicable VCoin or network verification events.

## 14. VACO INTEROPERABILITY GATEWAY — VIG

The network-facing doorway between VACO and external systems. **VIG
should NOT attempt to replace every external financial or business
network**; it allows VACO to communicate with and orchestrate
appropriate external systems.

FINANCIAL: banks, ACH, wire networks, payment processors, card
networks, international payment networks, regional payment systems,
other legally available financial infrastructure.
BUSINESS: POS, ERP, accounting, ecommerce, inventory, CRM, payroll,
invoicing, business applications.
LOGISTICS: shipping, warehousing, delivery, VOID systems, autonomous
delivery infrastructure.
IDENTITY / COMPLIANCE: KYB providers, identity providers, licensing
systems, compliance providers.
DIGITAL ASSETS: VOKEN, external blockchain networks, custody systems,
asset registries.

## 15. VACO BUSINESS NETWORK SDK

The developer-facing doorway into VACO. An outside business should be
able to integrate similarly to major infrastructure APIs. **The SDK
should expose existing VACO capabilities rather than recreate them.**

Potential functionality: VACA verification, Business Passport, business
identity, wallets, VCoin, VASH, V3, VOKEN, contracts, escrow,
settlement, business relationships, network events, compliance status,
audit records, webhooks, transaction status.

Initial language/platform priorities determined by the existing VACO
technology stack. Potential future SDKs: JavaScript/TypeScript, Python,
others based on actual developer demand.

## 16. DEVELOPER PORTAL

Business verification, API keys, API scopes, permissions,
documentation, sandbox, test credentials, webhooks, API usage, network
activity, transaction monitoring, integration status, compliance
status. External businesses should be able to connect existing software
rather than replace it.

## 17. MULTI-TENANT ARCHITECTURE

**A foundational requirement.** Every business must have logical and
technical isolation for data, employees, roles, permissions, wallets,
treasury, transactions, tokens, assets, contracts, API credentials and
audit records.

Authorized network transactions must cross tenant boundaries without
exposing unauthorized information. Must scale 100 → 1,000 → 10,000 →
significantly larger.

## 18. NETWORK PERMISSION ENGINE

A centralized permission engine controlling the five levels. Level 1
permissions must not automatically grant Level 4 or 5 capabilities.
Advancement depends on verification, required documentation, business
eligibility, compliance requirements, appropriate approvals and
required controls. **Do not use arbitrary reputation scoring as the
sole mechanism for advancement.**

## 19. VACO COMMUNITY COMMERCE PROGRAM

Qualifying VACO Community Stores shall allocate a defined percentage of
eligible proceeds to community benefit within a defined geographic
service area.

INITIAL TARGET: **25% COMMUNITY ALLOCATION.**

The precise financial definition of the 25% must be established
operationally and legally. **Do not automatically assume the percentage
is 25% of gross revenue.** The exact eligible economic base must be
configurable and documented.

## 20. COMMUNITY STORE MODEL

Next-generation community commerce hubs: convenience retail, food,
prepared meals, fuel where applicable, EV charging, package services,
VOID delivery, local marketplace, AI-assisted shopping, VACO
wallet/payment, community services, local events, other approved
services.

Each qualifying Community Store establishes a defined local service
area. INITIAL TARGET: **FIVE-MILE RADIUS**, accounting for NORTH,
SOUTH, EAST and WEST from each participating location.

## 21. VOID HUB COMMUNITY DIVIDEND

The same model applies to qualifying VOID Hubs. EVERY QUALIFYING VOID
HUB establishes its own local community allocation. INITIAL TARGET:
**25% COMMUNITY ALLOCATION**, **FIVE-MILE RADIUS**.

Each VOID Hub must have its own identifiable Community Treasury and
Community Proof-of-Impact Ledger. Funds must remain traceable to the
originating hub and reporting period. **Do not invisibly pool separate
hub allocations** unless a documented community program explicitly
authorizes pooling.

## 22. COMMUNITY TREASURY

Each qualifying Community Store and VOID Hub has an independently
identifiable Community Treasury tracking: source location, reporting
period, eligible economic base, required allocation, amount allocated,
amount distributed, remaining balance, program/project, recipient
category, distribution date, transaction/reference ID, verification
status.

## 23. COMMUNITY PROOF-OF-IMPACT LEDGER

A public-facing transparency architecture showing how community
allocations are used: Store/Hub ID, reporting period, eligible economic
activity, community allocation, amount distributed, amount remaining,
program/project category, geographic service area, distribution date,
blockchain transaction/reference, verification status.

Residents should be able to understand: HOW MUCH WAS GENERATED → HOW
MUCH WAS ALLOCATED → WHERE IT WENT → WHEN IT WAS DISTRIBUTED → WHICH
COMMUNITY PROGRAM RECEIVED IT.

## 24. BLOCKCHAIN COMMUNITY TRANSPARENCY

Blockchain may provide tamper-evident verification of community
allocations and distributions. **Do NOT place sensitive personal
information on a public blockchain.** Use hashing, anonymous
identifiers, aggregation, transaction references, public proofs and
controlled off-chain documentation.

Example public record: Community Treasury #001 / Allocation $XX,XXX /
Distribution $X,XXX / Program: Youth Development / Transaction:
Blockchain Reference #XXXX / Verification: CONFIRMED. Actual
confidential recipient documentation remains in controlled VACO
systems.

BUSINESS ACTIVITY.

Maximize qualified, successful, economically productive businesses.
