# How Many Companies? It Depends Where You Cut

The master register counted **64**. That was one level of granularity,
and the challenge to it is fair: VOID is not one business, it is 25.
Vvltvre is five. VACAY is five. The systems layer is a stack of
Google- and Meta-scale products.

So here is the count at three levels, with the real numbers from code.
Which one is "right" is a structuring decision, not a fact — but the
decision should be made against real numbers.

---

## Level 1 — Holding and arms: **3**

VVI · VEGA · VEDA

The minimum viable structure. Everything else is divisions inside
three entities.

---

## Level 2 — Named businesses: **64**

What the master register enumerates. VOID counts once, Vvltvre counts
five times (its divisions are genuinely separate operations), the 13
fashion houses and 10 food brands count individually because each is a
distinct brand with its own storefront.

This is the level at which a business has **a name a customer would
recognise**.

## Which number to structure against

**Not 180.** One hundred and eighty entities means 180 sets of filings,
books, returns, and registered agents, plus intercompany agreements for
every flow between them. The administrative cost would exceed the
revenue of most of the lines being protected.

**Not 3 either.** Three entities means a restaurant's food-safety
liability, a brokerage's regulatory exposure, and a gaming operator's
licensing all sit in the same balance sheet.

**The useful frame:** 180 is the *operating* reality — it is what the
business actually does, and it is the right number for planning,
staffing, and P&L. Somewhere between 8 and 15 is the likely *entity*
count, formed where liability, regulation, or a plan to sell
separately actually justifies one.

The register at Level 2 (64) is the menu you choose from. The
compliance register says which ones are forced: VEX, VAGO, V3, YAP,
VAZAN, the food group, and VOID's licensing-gated verticals.

## One thing this makes obvious

At Level 3, **VOID alone is 25 businesses** — more than most holding
companies own in total. VDP is another 22. VOKEN is 22.

Those three parents contain 69 operating lines between them, which is
more than the entire Level 2 count of the ecosystem. The structure
discussion has been treating them as three names on a list; they are
each a conglomerate in their own right.
